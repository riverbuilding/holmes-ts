01 / The workflow

From a question
to a grounded answer.

Holmes is designed around a simple principle: observations come first, conclusions come second.

  1. 01

    Investigate

    Ask a focused question about a pinned local Docker context or a deterministic fixture.

    ⌕
  2. 02

    Collect

    Use a focused toolkit to inspect containers, logs, events, images, processes, history, and filesystem changes.

    ⊞
  3. 03

    Explain

    Receive a finding, next steps, uncertainty, and citations to the exact retained evidence.

    ↗
02 / Built with boundaries

Useful because it’s
deliberately limited.

◌

Read-only by design

Holmes investigates. It never runs docker exec, changes containers, or applies a fix on your behalf.

✦

Evidence, not raw output

Bounded observations receive stable IDs. Conclusions can be checked against the evidence that supports them.

⌁

Secrets stay redacted

Known secret values are removed before evidence is retained, counted, or returned in an answer.

⌛

Bounded at every turn

Deadlines, per-operation timeouts, output caps, and duplicate-call suppression keep investigations controlled.
